## 👋 About Me

I'm a full-stack developer who enjoys building software from the ground up — turning a blank screen into something people actually use.

Most of my time goes into building **SaaS products**, **AI agents**, **CRM systems**, and **startup MVPs**. I like solving backend problems, designing scalable architectures, and integrating AI into *real products*, not demos.

Outside client work, I'm constantly experimenting with new tech, shipping side projects, and finding faster ways to build software.
